Aquatics

Two city pools, one schedule. See what's in the water right now, at which pool, on how many lanes and until when — lap and gentle swim, water fitness, lessons and Masters.

Community Rec Center pool: temporarily closed. Lap Swim and Gentle Swim are at the Margarita Rec Center pool for now. No reopening date has been announced; the city's notice was last updated October 15.

Live from the city's posted schedule and class catalog. Lane assignments are our estimate where the city doesn't publish them.

The two pools

Margarita Rec Center · 29119 Margarita Rd. · 25 yards, 6 lanes, warm water (about 85°), zero-depth entry, jump board, in-water wheelchair.

Ronald J. Parks Community Rec Center · 30875 Rancho Vista Rd. · 25 yards, 6 lanes, heated to about 80°, slide, splash pad; diving board closed for repairs.

Programs

Lap & Gentle Swim

Drop-in. $1 Temecula residents, $5 non-residents. Lap swim 12 and up; Gentle Swim 18 and up, in the warm water.

Water fitness

Water Walking, Adaptive Water Exercise, Aqua Fit, Deep and Shallow Water Aerobics. Pre-registration required.

Swim lessons

Parent & Me from 6 months, toddler, Levels 1–3, teen, adult and private lessons. Pre-registration required.

Masters

Speed & Endurance (Mon/Wed/Fri) and Skill & Technique (Tue/Thu/Sat), $30 a month, at the Community Rec Center.

Public swim & Family Night

Summer season. No fall public swim is posted at either pool.

  • Times live somewhere else. The Water Exercise page names nine classes and gives no times or pool for any of them; those are only in the registration catalog.
  • Two answers for Family Night. The Public Swim page runs Monday Family Night at Margarita “thru Sep 14”; the Family Night page runs it to October 12.
  • One schedule instead. Each program is entered once. This page, both pool calendars and the “now” board update together, and the closure ends on its own date the day it's known.
